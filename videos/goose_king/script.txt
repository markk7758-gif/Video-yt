This king surrendered his entire kingdom... to a goose.

It started at his birthday party, when a goose bit him right on the bottom. King Edwin decided: never again.

So he builds a wall. Then a bigger wall around that wall.
Then a moat. Then a moat around the moat, filled with eels, in case the goose could swim. It could.
Then he raises the drawbridge and melts down the chains, so nothing can ever get in again.

Luckily, nothing can get in. Unluckily, that includes food.

See, a castle is basically a giant lunchbox. It only works if someone keeps putting lunch in it.

Week one, they eat the bread. Week two, boot soup. Week three, the chef is fishing eels out of the moat... and the eels are fishing back.

Week six, a guard spots something on the horizon. Not an army. One goose.

Then winter freezes both moats solid. The goose waddles across the ice, squeezes through an arrow slit, and sits down on the throne.

The starving king takes one look at it... and hands over the crown.

Historians call it the shortest invasion in history. The goose just calls it... honk.
