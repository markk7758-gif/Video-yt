# Wojtek: facts and sources

- Syrian brown bear cub bought in 1942 in Iran (near Hamadan) from a local boy by Polish soldiers of Anders' Army, recently released from Soviet camps. (Imperial War Museum, "Wojtek the bear"; Aileen Orr, *Wojtek the Bear: Polish War Hero*, 2010)
- Fed condensed milk from an empty vodka bottle; later drank beer and ate cigarettes; wrestled with soldiers. (IWM; BBC News, "Wojtek the bear", 2015)
- Officially enlisted (rank, serial number) in the 22nd Artillery Supply Company, Polish II Corps, so he could sail to Italy, where ships did not carry animals. (IWM; BBC)
- Battle of Monte Cassino, 1944: soldiers reported he carried crates of artillery shells. Some historians doubt it, so the script says "the soldiers swear". (IWM; BBC)
- The company badge became a bear carrying a shell; he was promoted to corporal. (IWM; BBC)
- After the war Poland was under Soviet control and many of the men could not return. Wojtek went to Scotland, then lived in Edinburgh Zoo from 1947 until he died in 1963 (16 years). Polish veterans visited and talked to him in Polish. (Edinburgh Zoo / RZSS; BBC)
